# Third-party notices

Original Ingestron code and third-party content have separate rights. Preparing
an Apache licence for original code does not relicense bundled dependencies.

Exact installed production packages and their retained licence/notice files are
recorded in `licensing/dependency-inventory.json` and `licensing/third-party/`.
Missing or ambiguous notices require review before public release. This inventory
is technical evidence, not a legal opinion or a claim of complete rights clearance.

Bundled assets additionally retain:

- ODCS schema: `schemas/ODCS-LICENSE` and `schemas/provenance.json` (copied unchanged
  into distribution assets).
- Meltano catalogue metadata: `catalogue/MELTANO-LICENSE.txt` and
  `catalogue/NOTICE.txt`. Catalogue presence does not grant rights to individual
  connector implementations, endorse them or promise their compatibility.
